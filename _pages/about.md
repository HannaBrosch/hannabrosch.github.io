Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://www.wipo.wiso.rw.fau.de/). I received my PhD in Economics from the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.mgt.tum.de/global-center-for-family-enterprise/), where I was supervised by [Philipp Lergetporer](https://sites.google.com/lergetporer.at/philipplergetporereconomics). My research interests lie at the intersection of Gender and Labor Economics, Education, and Experimental Economics.

I completed my MSc in Economics at LMU Munich in 2022. In Fall 2024, I was a visiting PhD student at Stanford Graduate School of Education, where I was hosted by Eric Bettinger.

If you are interested in my research or would like to chat, feel free to reach out!
